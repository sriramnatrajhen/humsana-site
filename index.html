Humsana binds authority to a consequential action, checks that the action reaching execution is still within that authority, and refuses when it is not.

A refusal names the field, both values and the rule. It runs where the integrating party places it, and it spends nothing.

The payment was authorized. The account was not. Every step in the chain looks approved, and the money still leaves for somewhere nobody approved.

This is the class behind business email compromise, and behind every payment instruction that was genuine until one field changed.
