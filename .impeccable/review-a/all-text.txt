Humsana makes AI agents accountable for the actions they take.

Inside your own stack, identity, policy and application controls can govern what an agent does. The hard part begins when the agent leaves it. Humsana binds authority to the action, checks that binding where the action reaches its executor, and stops execution when the action is not covered.

The execution boundary.

Humsana is not trying to replace the identity system, the agent runtime or the executor. It sits at the point where a consequential action crosses from one authority context into another and asks whether the authority actually covers what is being executed.

Where it does not, the action is stopped before the executor reaches the system that would perform it, and the refusal names the field, both values and the rule.

What a refusal says.

Every refusal carries a code. The code names which check failed and, where a field was involved, which field carried the difference.

CODE	WHAT FAILED
EFFECTUATION_DRIFT	The presented action differs from the authorized action in at least one material field. The refusal carries the field, the authorized value, the presented value and the rule that was applied.
MAY_NOT_CHANGE	The field is fixed by the authorization and cannot be altered at execution.
MAY_NOT_INCREASE	The field may be reduced but not raised above the authorized value.
MAY_ONLY_SHRINK	The scope may narrow but not widen.
GRANT_DOES_NOT_COVER_THIS_ACTION	The presented action is outside what this authorization covers.
GRANT_ALREADY_USED	The grant has been spent. A grant is spent once.
GRANT_NOT_FOUND	No grant exists for the identifier presented.
AUTHORIZATION_NOT_GRANTED	There is no authorization to spend. A receipt that asked for verification, or that was refused, answers this and no other code, because it was never spendable and so cannot have expired.
CONFIRMATION_NOT_OBTAINED	The action requires a human step and no bound approval was presented.
RECEIPT_EXPIRED	The authorization is past its expiry. It applies to an authorization that existed.
RECEIPT_NOT_FOUND	Unknown identifier, or one that belongs to another caller.
OUTSIDE_SCOPE	The action falls outside the scope the principal registered for this agent.
PROVIDER_DEADLINE_EXCEEDED	A provider did not answer inside the deadline. The boundary waits up to twenty seconds and records the expiry rather than treating silence as consent.

Thirteen codes are shown. The boundary answers in these plus the class and registry checks the deployment has enabled.

Where the boundary sits.
WHO AUTHENTICATES THE APPROVING HUMAN
the principal's identity system
NOT HUMSANA

Humsana verifies the authenticated identity or assertion the customer supplies, records who was authenticated and by which method, and binds the confirmation to the action. Authentication itself belongs to the principal's identity system. A production integration accepts that system's identity, assertion or signature; the sandbox uses a reference confirmer and labels it as one.

WHO OWNS THE EXECUTOR
the integrating party
ENFORCED AT THE SEAM

Humsana enforces at the execution seam controlled by the integrating party. We cannot enforce an executor we do not control. The reference executor exists to demonstrate the seam and to be replaced by yours.
