# Surface brief: the website (Home, The execution boundary)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a risk, compliance or platform decision-maker at a payment institution or platform,
plus the engineer who would integrate. Job: make the niche obvious within seconds, prove it with the refusal,
then say honestly what is still unvalidated.

## Direction contract

THESIS. The execution boundary is the product's subject and the page's structure. The page refuses the category
default of a centred hero over feature cards: it is a two-region document with one boundary running through it,
because the product's only claim is about what happens where two systems meet.

OWN-WORLD. Paper ground #F7F7F3, ink #171717, secondary #66707A, rule #D9D9D2, signal #D96B32 used only for a
changed value and a refusal, pale signal #F3E4DA. Two regions separated by a 2px ink boundary rule: the left on
paper, the right on a half-step warmer neutral so "another system" is legible without colour noise. IBM Plex
Sans for text, IBM Plex Mono for identifiers, codes, digests and counts. Hairline rules carry structure; no
cards, no shadows, no radius on containers.

STORY. A visitor understands in the first viewport that an agent's authority and the executor's system are no
longer inside one boundary. They believe it because the boundary switch shows the facts the executor loses as
the boundary hardens, and because the refusal quotes the live service. They act by opening the sandbox.

FIRST VIEWPORT. Left, two thirds: the thesis line, the question "Who can prove what that agent was actually
authorized to do?", one line of mechanism, and the primary action "See it refuse". Right, one third: the
boundary diagram, Company A above the rule and Company B below it, in its hardest case, with the three-case
switch beneath it. Signature interaction: the boundary switch, which redraws the rule and moves the executor's
known facts into unknowns across the three cases.

FORM. Established world, extended. No concept roll: the brief specifies the structure, so this is shaped
directly and built code-led, no comp round (no image generation in this environment).

FINISH. unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md,
and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
