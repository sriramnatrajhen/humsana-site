01Trust

What we claim, and what we do not.

Every number carries its method. Every gap is stated here rather than found later.

02Measurements

The method behind each figure.

  • Authorization and execution cost: 5.8 ms median to authorize, 2.9 ms to enforce, worst case 12.9 ms over 40 actions. Measured on the current single-host deployment, with no network hop and no load generator. A cost check, not a benchmark.
  • Agent variance: seven runs of one workflow produced seven distinct artifacts, with an identical recipient set in 21 of 21 comparisons and no amount differing. The decision was stable. The bytes were not. That is why a digest is bound, and why the approved artifact should be archived and the archive executed.
  • Refusals and messages: 42 cross-run presentations refused before the send API, 0 matched, 0 grants consumed. An executor with no boundary really sent 14 messages. One task, one provider, one day.
  • Noise gate: at 30 dB high-band SNR, affirmative clearances fell from 64 to 0 as noise was added. Noisy audio is never cleared.
03Not claimed

Stated plainly.

  • No SOC 2, no ISO 27001, no third-party audit.
  • No high availability. One small host today.
  • No paying customers, and no production deployment handling consequential actions for a customer.
  • No verification of a presented delegation. Scope, revocation and expiry are not enforced yet.
  • No named customer, and no claim that an external party already accepts our receipts.
  • Swan is not presented as interview-ready. It has a coverage limit, and degraded audio is one.
04Data

What is stored, and what is not.

  • Each record holds the claim, what was established, the grant with its bound digests, the confirmation and its approver, and the receipt.
  • The parameters of the authorized action are stored, because that is what makes a field comparable later.
  • Voice audio is processed to return a result and not stored. Request metadata is kept for 30 days.
  • The sandbox runs on generated data, with its own register and no production credentials.
05Alongside, not instead

Where it sits with your controls.

Screening, monitoring and name checking produce results. Humsana consumes those results and binds them into the thing it enforces, so a result that changes after approval is refused like any other field. It does not produce them, and it does not replace them.

We are not claiming to satisfy any regulatory requirement. We are claiming that the principle they share, an approval bound to the exact action, is buildable today for actions no person performs.

06Evaluate us

Three things to check without our help.

  • Run the sandbox, change the instruction, and read the field, both values and the rule it names.
  • Ask what a receipt records, what it does not, and who would accept it today.
  • Ask what is unverified. Delegation verification is the clearest example, and the record says so.
