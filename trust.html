02Measurements

The method behind each figure.

Authorization and execution cost

5.8 ms median to authorize one exact action and 2.9 ms median to enforce the binding at execution, worst case 12.9 ms across a set of 40 actions. Measured on the current single-host deployment, with the boundary and the service on the same machine as the measurement harness. No network hop, no load generator, no production traffic. It is a cost check, not a benchmark.

Agent variance, seven runs

One autonomous workflow, run seven times against the same task and the same portfolio, produced seven distinct artifacts. The recipient set was identical in 21 of 21 comparisons and no amount or account differed, so the decision was stable. The bytes were not. That finding is why the boundary binds a digest of the artifact rather than a description of it, and why the guidance is to archive the approved artifact and execute the archive.

Forty-two refusals, and fourteen messages that were sent

Presenting each run's artifact against another run's approval produced 42 refusals before the send API was reached, with 0 matched and 0 grants consumed. In the same experiment, an executor with no boundary in front of it really sent 14 messages. One task, one portfolio, one provider, one day. Not a benchmark, and not a claim about production traffic.

Swan's noise gate

A gate on high-band signal-to-noise is part of the voice path, at 30 dB. In a sweep, affirmative clearances fell from 64 to 0 as noise was added, and the pre-change baseline of 75 clearances went to 0. Noisy audio is never cleared affirmatively. That is a guardrail, not an accuracy claim.

03Not claimed

Stated plainly.

  • No SOC 2 report and no ISO 27001 certification.
  • No third-party security audit or penetration test.
  • No high-availability deployment. Today this runs on a single small host.
  • No paying customers yet, and no production deployment handling consequential actions on a customer's behalf.
  • No verification of a presented delegation. A delegation that arrives with a request is recorded as unverified, and delegation scope, revocation and expiry are not enforced yet.
  • No Pesa integration, and no claim that any named company uses this.
  • No claim that an external party already accepts Humsana receipts as evidence of authorization. That is a conversation, not a shipped fact.
  • Swan is not presented as interview-ready. Voice integrity has a coverage limit, and degraded audio is one of them.

Where a claim here is load-bearing for a decision you are making, ask us for the run, the dates and the raw output behind it. If we cannot produce it, treat the claim as unproven.

04Data

What is stored, and what is not.

Authorization records

For each action, the boundary stores the claim, what it established, the grant with its bound field digests, the confirmation and its approver, and the receipt. These records are the audit artifact, and they are kept because they are the product.

Action parameters

The parameters of the action being authorized are stored inside the record. That is what makes a bound field comparable later. A deployment decides what its action parameters may contain.

Voice

The voice screening service processes the audio to return a result. The audio is not stored. Metadata about the request is retained for 30 days.

The sandbox

Generated data. No personal data, no customer data, no real money, and a separate register from production.

05Existing controls

Alongside, not instead of.

Sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, name checking and fraud controls produce results. Humsana consumes those results and binds them into the thing it enforces, so a result that changes after approval is a change to the action and is refused like any other. It does not produce those results, and it is not a replacement for any of them.

The regulatory context we read carefully, without claiming that it requires us: the FCA's 2026 Mills Review on autonomous AI and accountability; strong customer authentication rules that require dynamic linking of certain payments to the amount and the payee, with invalidation when either changes; and Confirmation of Payee, which exists precisely because a payee change is its own control point.

We are not claiming to satisfy any of these requirements. We are claiming that the principle they share, an approval bound to the exact action, is buildable today for actions no person performs.

06Evaluate us

Four things you can check without our help.

Run the sandbox

It is a live deployment. Change the instruction, watch the refusal, and read the field, both values and the rule it names.

Ask for a receipt

Ask what a receipt records and what it does not, and ask who would accept it today. The honest answer is on this page.

Ask what is unverified

Delegation verification is the clearest example. If the boundary cannot establish something, it says so, and the record says so. Test that claim against a request that asserts authority it cannot prove.

Ask about the deployment

Today: one small host, no high availability, credentials issued by an operator rather than self-serve, and TLS with a 1.2 floor. A pilot means a real integration with your rail and your identity system, not a hosted version of the sandbox.

Ask us the hard question first.

If something on this site is wrong, we would rather hear it from you than have it found later.
