01Mechanism

From authority to action.

What the caller must assert, and what the boundary refuses to infer from it.

02The request

An agent presents a claim, not a proof.

  • Who is asking. The agent identifies itself and the organization it acts for.
  • What it wants to do. The action arrives as structured fields, not as a sentence.
  • What authority it claims. A mandate or an upstream approval. A presented delegation is recorded as unverified today.
  • What consequence it carries. The declaration is recorded and can never lower what the class requires.
03Evidence

Five states, never blended.

  • Measured. The boundary's own machinery produced this.
  • Verified. An external check confirmed it, and the record names which one.
  • Reported. A party said so, and it is repeated as a report.
  • Unverified. Nothing supports it. This is most of what an agent asserts.
  • Unknown. Nothing was established and nothing was claimed. Silence, not consent.

Measured outranks asserted, which outranks silence. A claim can make a decision stricter. It can never make one more permissive.

04Consequence

The action decides the floor.

  • Each action class carries a floor: how much evidence, and whether a person must confirm.
  • Reversible actions stay cheap. A read or a draft needs no confirmation.
  • Irreversible actions floor at a person, by construction, not by the model's confidence.
05Confirmation

The approval is an act, not a flag.

  • The approver sees the fields that will be bound, and approves that instruction as it stands.
  • The approval is spent once, and it expires. A late approval is refused by the boundary, not by the rail.
  • Humsana does not authenticate the approver. That belongs to the customer's identity system.
06The grant

Single use, exact action.

  • Every bound field is named with its digest. A changed byte is a changed field.
  • A bound set may shrink. It may not grow. Adding one recipient after approval is refused.
  • A refusal carries the field, both values and the rule, as a structure rather than a sentence.
07Execution

The check happens where the action happens.

  • The executor presents the action and its grant immediately before the send, the payment or the deletion.
  • A field that moved stops the action, and the grant is not consumed.
  • A second presentation of the same grant is refused, so a retry cannot release twice.
  • The rail is the customer's. Making it require a grant is the integration.
08The provider

Models advise. Policy decides.

  • The deployment runs deterministically by default, and ships that way.
  • Where a model is enabled it can only ask for a stricter outcome, and its output has no field that could carry a decision.
  • The boundary waits for a provider only to its own deadline, and records a timeout by name.
09The calls

Four calls, and a refusal you can read.

POST /v1/authorize/verify decide whether this exact action may proceed POST /v1/authorize/confirm/request ask the authenticated approver POST /v1/authorize/confirm record that approval against that instruction POST /v1/authorize/effectuate release, only if the arriving action matches the grant
