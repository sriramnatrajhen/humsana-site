Regulation

Where the principle comes from

The FCA's 2026 Mills Review discusses the move from AI assistance to AI delegation, and the need for clear accountability, audit trails, explainable outcomes and meaningful human approval. fca.org.uk

Strong customer authentication, RTS Article 5, requires certain remote payments to be dynamically linked to the amount and the payee. Change either and the authentication is invalid.

Confirmation of Payee, Specific Direction 17, applies when a new payee is added or an existing payee's details change. wearepay.uk

Humsana applies the same shape to autonomous execution: make the approval explicit, bind it to the action, and check it again when the action happens. We are not claiming to satisfy any of these requirements.

Measurement

Authorization cost

5.8 ms median to authorize one exact action, 2.9 ms median to check it at execution, worst case 12.9 ms over a run of 40 actions, on the current single-host deployment. The work is a deterministic comparison of bound fields, not a model call.

Agent variance

Seven runs of one autonomous task, on one provider, in one day. Seven distinct artifacts. The recipient set was the same every time. All 42 presentations were refused before the send API. Zero matched, zero grants consumed, and 14 messages that the unwatched executor really sent.

Voice

The noise gate is set at 30 dB. Across the evaluation, synthetic detections that became affirmatives fell from 64 to 0. Audio below the floor returns inconclusive rather than cleared.

None of this is a benchmark. It is what the current deployment and the current test corpora produced. The full method, the corpora and the failures are in the repository.

02Data

What is kept, and for how long.

A record holds

What the caller claimed, what was established, the grant with the digest of every bound field, the confirmation with its approver, and the receipt for what executed or was refused.

Voice audio

Processed to reach a verdict and not stored. Where a voice check is used at all, the evidence contributes a state. It cannot clear a caller on its own.

Metadata

Retained for 30 days on the current deployment. The retention policy changes when this runs inside your environment, because the store does.

The sandbox

Uses generated data. No real money moves, and no bank is involved at any point in the demonstration.

03Evaluate us

Three things to ask for.

  • Run the sandbox and try to make a changed field pass. Nothing you can do makes it pass.
  • Ask for a receipt and for who would accept it. The record is a structure, not a log line.
  • Ask what is unverified. Delegation scope, revocation and expiry are not enforced today, and this page says so above.
