What happens

Four fields, four outcomes.

The approved action is a payment: £1,800 to Supplier X, account 4821, valid until 14:00.

Change the amount, the beneficiary, the account or the expiry. The page presents the action to the live service and shows the answer it returns.

Restore the approved value and the same action is allowed, once.

What the sandbox is.

No login. No persistent state. Synthetic data only, and no real money moves.

The refusal you see is the service's own answer, not a scripted screen.

Where a confirmation is shown, the sandbox uses a reference confirmer and labels it. A production integration accepts the principal's authenticated identity, assertion or signature.
