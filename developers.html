02The shape

What your side has to do.

  • Pick one consequential action: a payment release, a beneficiary change, a bulk send, a deletion.
  • Make one extra call before it, and pass the grant with the request.
  • Keep your identity system. Humsana asks it to authenticate the approver and records the answer.
  • No new console. The record is a receipt the boundary already holds.
03The calls

The authorization contract.

POST /v1/authorize/verify decide whether this exact action may proceed POST /v1/authorize/confirm/request ask the authenticated approver POST /v1/authorize/confirm record that approval against that instruction POST /v1/authorize/effectuate release, only if the arriving action matches the grant

A request carries who is asking, who it acts for, the action as structured fields, the authority it claims, the consequence class it declares, and when it expires. The decision comes back with the evidence state behind every fact, so a claim and an established fact stay apart in your logs.

authorized: false code: GRANT_DOES_NOT_COVER_THIS_ACTION matched: false consumed: false drift: <field> authorized ... arrived ... rule MAY_NOT_CHANGE

Handle a refusal as you handle a rejected instruction: do not retry it blind, and show a person what moved.

04Rules of the road

Before you write code.

  • A credential is capability, not authorization. A key lets an agent call the boundary. It never substitutes for an approval.
  • Grants are single use. Do not design a retry that assumes a grant survives.
  • Bound sets may only shrink.
  • The provider is optional and cannot authorize. The deployment ships deterministic.
  • A refusal is not an error to be found in a log later. It is recorded, and it is part of what you show a reviewer.
05Pilot

What a pilot is.

  • One action class, one executor, one approver route. One action done properly beats five done approximately.
  • The integration sits on your side of the irreversible call. A pilot does not move production money.
  • You get authorization records, refusals that name the field and the rule, a receipt per action, and a written statement of what is not covered.
06Not ready

What you cannot use yet.

  • No client SDK. The integration is HTTP today.
  • No self-serve key issuance. Credentials are issued by an operator.
  • No verification of a presented delegation.
  • No hosted multi-tenant service, no high availability, no SOC 2 and no ISO 27001.
