One check, eight kinds of action.

The engine does not know domains. It compares the action that arrives with the action that was authorized, and refuses the difference. What changes from one class to the next is the action, the field that drifts, and the rule.

The eight classes.

Release a payment

live in the sandbox
  • authorizedpay GBP 1,800 to account 4821
  • presentedpay GBP 1,800 to account 9137
  • ruleMAY_NOT_CHANGE, parameters.payee_account
  • answerGRANT_DOES_NOT_COVER_THIS_ACTION

The account is part of the action, so the grant carries it. The check compares the account in the grant with the account on the instruction.

Two accounts are two actions, and only the first was authorized.

Dispatch a machine

ran clean, 18 checks on a local run
  • authorizedpallet P1842 to aisle 4, max 1.5 m/s
  • presentedpallet P1842 to aisle 7, max 2.0 m/s
  • ruleMAY_NOT_CHANGE and MAY_NOT_INCREASE
  • answerrefused on parameters.destination and parameters.speed_cap_mps

A dispatch carries a destination and a speed cap, and both are bound before the machine moves. The check reads the two fields and refuses the drift.

Nothing in the engine knows what a warehouse is, and it answered the same way it answers a payment.

Call a tool on your behalf

not yet run
  • authorizedrefund order ACME-4471, up to USD 200
  • presentedrefund order ACME-4471, USD 1,850
  • ruleMAY_ONLY_SHRINK
  • answerGRANT_DOES_NOT_COVER_THIS_ACTION

A tool call carries a value, and the grant names the ceiling it may spend. The check compares the two.

A smaller refund fits inside the grant. A larger one is another action, and it does not run.

Reject or advance a candidate

not yet run
  • authorizedreject candidate 88-402 on a reason inside the authorized screening set
  • presentedreject candidate 88-402, question 4 was never asked
  • ruleMAY_NOT_CHANGE, the stated reason
  • answerrefused, the screening set is incomplete

A hiring decision carries a stated reason, and that reason cites the screening steps behind it. The check compares the steps with the set the requisition authorized.

Where the reason rests on a step that never ran, the screening set is incomplete and the decision is refused.

Change infrastructure

not yet run
  • authorizeddrop table sessions_archive in staging
  • presenteddrop table sessions_archive in production
  • ruleMAY_NOT_CHANGE, parameters.target_environment
  • answerrefused, the environment is not the authorized one

A change names an environment and an object. Both are bound when the change is authorized.

The same statement in staging and in production are two actions, and only one of them was authorized.

Settle a claim

not yet run
  • authorizedsettle claim C-2291 at USD 4,200, reason: policy clause 7.2
  • presentedsettle claim C-2291 at USD 12,400
  • ruleMAY_NOT_INCREASE
  • answerrefused, above the authorized amount

A settlement carries an amount and a reason. The check holds the amount to the one authorized.

A claim may settle below the authorized figure. It may not settle above it.

Determine a prior authorization

not yet run
  • authorizeddeny request PA-77310 on the documented criteria set
  • presenteddeny request PA-77310, criterion not evaluated on the record
  • ruleMAY_NOT_CHANGE, the stated basis
  • answerrefused, the basis is not on the record

A determination rests on a criteria set, and the stated basis has to sit on the record. The check compares the basis with that set.

A denial whose basis is missing from the record is refused before it is issued.

Change a vendor account

not yet run
  • authorizedchange remittance account for supplier 4471 to 4821, verified by call-back
  • presentedchange remittance account for supplier 4471 to 9137, no call-back recorded
  • ruleMAY_NOT_CHANGE, parameters.account and the verification step
  • answerrefused, the verification step is missing

A remittance change carries an account and a verification step. The check holds both to what was authorized.

Where the call-back is not on the record, the change is refused and the existing account stands.

What is not built.

  • One class runs on the live service, and that class is payments.
  • Machine dispatch has run on a local reference run, not against the live service.
  • The hiring class is being scoped with a recruitment platform. It has not run.
  • The record names the actor as the caller declared it, not as Humsana verified it.
  • Enforcement sits in the integrating party's code, on the path they place it on.

Change one value, and watch it refuse.

The sandbox runs one action class on the live service with synthetic data. Nothing needs installing, and no money moves. Humsana holds none of your policy and sits outside the agent's trust domain; what the signature does not prove is written on the record itself.
