One check, applied to eight kinds of action.

The engine holds no knowledge of any domain. It compares the action arriving at execution against the action that was authorized and refuses the difference, which is why one check covers a payment, a machine dispatch, a claim or a hiring decision.

The eight classes.

Release a payment

live in the sandbox
  • authorizedpay GBP 1,800 to account 4821
  • presentedpay GBP 1,800 to account 9137
  • ruleMAY_NOT_CHANGE, parameters.payee_account
  • answerGRANT_DOES_NOT_COVER_THIS_ACTION

Dispatch a machine

  • authorizedpallet P1842 to aisle 4, max 1.5 m/s
  • presentedpallet P1842 to aisle 7, max 2.0 m/s
  • ruleMAY_NOT_CHANGE and MAY_NOT_INCREASE
  • answerrefused on parameters.destination and parameters.speed_cap_mps

Call a tool on your behalf

  • authorizedrefund order ACME-4471, up to USD 200
  • presentedrefund order ACME-4471, USD 1,850
  • ruleMAY_ONLY_SHRINK
  • answerGRANT_DOES_NOT_COVER_THIS_ACTION

Reject or advance a candidate

  • authorizedreject candidate 88-402 on a reason inside the authorized screening set
  • presentedreject candidate 88-402, question 4 was never asked
  • ruleMAY_NOT_CHANGE, the stated reason
  • answerrefused, the screening set is incomplete

Change infrastructure

  • authorizeddrop table sessions_archive in staging
  • presenteddrop table sessions_archive in production
  • ruleMAY_NOT_CHANGE, parameters.target_environment
  • answerrefused, the environment is not the authorized one

Settle a claim

  • authorizedsettle claim C-2291 at USD 4,200, reason: policy clause 7.2
  • presentedsettle claim C-2291 at USD 12,400
  • ruleMAY_NOT_INCREASE
  • answerrefused, above the authorized amount

Determine a prior authorization

  • authorizeddeny request PA-77310 on the documented criteria set
  • presenteddeny request PA-77310, criterion not evaluated on the record
  • ruleMAY_NOT_CHANGE, the stated basis
  • answerrefused, the basis is not on the record

Change a vendor account

  • authorizedchange remittance account for supplier 4471 to 4821, verified by call-back
  • presentedchange remittance account for supplier 4471 to 9137, no call-back recorded
  • ruleMAY_NOT_CHANGE, parameters.account and the verification step
  • answerrefused, the verification step is missing

Change one value, and watch it refuse.

The sandbox runs one action class on the live service with synthetic data. Nothing needs installing, and no money moves. Humsana holds none of your policy and sits outside the agent's trust domain; what the signature does not prove is written on the record itself.
