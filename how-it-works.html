The mechanism

From authority to execution.

One action, followed the whole way. The payout below is the same action at every step.

01 / Authority

The principal grants authority.

A person or a company decides what an agent may do on its behalf.

Humsana records that grant. It does not create it, widen it or renew it.

From this point the question is not who the agent is. It is whether this action is inside the grant.

02 / Delegation

What the agent may do, and within what limits.

The grant names the action class, the limits and the period it is valid for.

A limit can be a maximum amount, a route, a destination, or a condition that has to hold when the action runs.

A grant is single use. Spending it consumes it.

03 / Action

The exact action, not the idea of the action.

An action is a set of values: what is being done, to whom, for how much, by when, and under what conditions.

Canonicalised, that set has one digest. The digest is what the approval signs and what the executor is checked against.

Change one value and the digest changes.

04 / Decision

The consequence decides whether a person has to approve.

Low-consequence actions can proceed on the grant alone. Higher-consequence actions require a human confirmation, and the confirmation signs the action itself rather than a flag on a request.

The approving human is authenticated by the principal's identity system. Humsana verifies what it is handed and records who approved, by what method, and over which exact action.

The decision is one of six: ALLOW, ALLOW WITH LIMITS, VERIFY, HUMAN CONFIRM, HOLD, DENY.

05 / Execution

The executor checks immediately before the action runs.

The executor presents the action to Humsana at the moment it is about to act.

Humsana compares the presented action against the authorized one and answers: ALLOWED on an exact match, ALLOWED WITH LIMITS on legitimate bounded variance, REFUSED on a material change.

A refusal does not consume the authorization. The approved action can still be executed as approved.

06 / Evidence

One record, from grant to execution.

The record keeps the states apart: what was claimed, what was established, what was authorized, what was presented, what executed, what was refused, and why.

It carries the policy version, the action digest, the approving identity and the timestamps.

A refusal stays in the record. A claim never becomes evidence because it was repeated.

Refusals

What a refusal says.

Every refusal carries a code. The code names which check failed and, where a field was involved, which field carried the difference.

CodeWhat failed
EFFECTUATION_DRIFTThe presented action differs from the authorized action in at least one material field. The refusal carries the field, the authorized value, the presented value and the rule that was applied.
MAY_NOT_CHANGEThe field is fixed by the authorization and cannot be altered at execution.
MAY_NOT_INCREASEThe field may be reduced but not raised beyond the authorized value.
MAY_ONLY_SHRINKThe field may only reduce the reach of the action that was authorized.
BINDING_MISMATCHThe binding digest presented at execution is not the digest that was approved.
RECEIPT_NOT_FOUNDThere is no authorization with that identifier.
RECEIPT_EXPIREDThe authorization was valid when it was issued and is not valid now.
GRANT_ALREADY_USEDThe authorization was spent. A second presentation does not spend it again.
GRANT_NOT_FOUNDThe grant behind the action cannot be found.
GRANT_DOES_NOT_COVER_THIS_ACTIONThe grant exists and does not cover the action that was presented.
AUTHORIZATION_NOT_GRANTEDThe stored decision is not one that grants anything. A match proves the action is the one that was asked about, never that it was permitted.
CONFIRMATION_NOT_OBTAINEDThe action required a human confirmation and no valid confirmation is bound to it.
ACTION_OUTSIDE_SCOPEThe action class is outside the scope the principal granted.
CONDITION_NOT_EVALUABLEA condition in the authorization cannot be evaluated deterministically, so it is not assumed to hold.
CLOCK_NOT_SUPPLIEDDeciding needs a time and none was supplied.
PROVIDER_DEADLINE_EXCEEDEDAn external check did not answer inside the boundary's deadline, so the boundary did not treat silence as consent.
BOUNDARY_STORE_UNAVAILABLEThe boundary could not read its own store, so it could not answer.

Codes are the names the service returns, read from the implementation.
