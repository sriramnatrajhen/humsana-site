From authority to execution.

The principal grants authority over one action. Humsana records it, binds it to the action, and the executor asks again immediately before the action happens.

Five steps, and the fifth is the one that matters.

The agent says who it is, who it acts for, what it wants to do, and what authority it has.

Humsana separates what is established from what is claimed and what remains unknown.

The consequence decides whether a person has to approve. Where one does, the approval is signed over the exact action.

The approval is tied to the action, its bound values and its expiry. It can be spent once.

The executor presents the action immediately before it runs. A material change is refused and the changed field is named.

What a refusal says.

Every refusal carries a code. The code names which check failed and, where a field was involved, which field carried the difference.

CodeWhat failed
EFFECTUATION_DRIFTThe presented action differs from the authorized action in at least one material field. The refusal carries the field, the authorized value, the presented value and the rule that was applied.
MAY_NOT_CHANGEThe field is fixed by the authorization and cannot be altered at execution.
MAY_NOT_INCREASEThe field may be reduced but not raised above the authorized value.
MAY_ONLY_SHRINKThe scope may narrow but not widen.
GRANT_DOES_NOT_COVER_THIS_ACTIONThe presented action is outside what this authorization covers.
GRANT_ALREADY_USEDThe grant has been spent. A grant is spent once.
GRANT_NOT_FOUNDNo grant exists for the identifier presented.
AUTHORIZATION_NOT_GRANTEDThere is no authorization to spend. A receipt that asked for verification, or that was refused, answers this and no other code, because it was never spendable and so cannot have expired.
CONFIRMATION_NOT_OBTAINEDThe action requires a human step and no bound approval was presented.
RECEIPT_EXPIREDThe authorization is past its expiry. It applies to an authorization that existed.
RECEIPT_NOT_FOUNDUnknown identifier, or one that belongs to another caller.
OUTSIDE_SCOPEThe action falls outside the scope the principal registered for this agent.
PROVIDER_DEADLINE_EXCEEDEDA provider did not answer inside the deadline. The boundary waits up to twenty seconds and records the expiry rather than treating silence as consent.

Thirteen codes are shown. The boundary answers in these plus the class and registry checks the deployment has enabled.

Where the boundary sits.

Who authenticates the approving human

the principal's identity system
not Humsana

Humsana verifies the authenticated identity or assertion the customer supplies, records who was authenticated and by which method, and binds the confirmation to the action. Authentication itself belongs to the principal's identity system. A production integration accepts that system's identity, assertion or signature; the sandbox uses a reference confirmer and labels it as one.

Who owns the executor

the integrating party
enforced at the seam

Humsana enforces at the execution seam controlled by the integrating party. We cannot enforce an executor we do not control. The reference executor exists to demonstrate the seam and to be replaced by yours.

The principal grants the authority. Humsana records it, binds it and enforces it.
