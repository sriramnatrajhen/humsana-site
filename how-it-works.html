The mechanism

From authority to execution.

One action, followed the whole way. The action below is a payout release, and the same six steps hold for any class the principal registers.

01 Claim

The agent says who it is, who it acts for, what it wants to do and what authority it has.

Everything that arrives from a caller is a claim until something establishes it.

Humsana records the claims as claims, and never promotes one to evidence because it was repeated.

  • Actoragent payout-repair-1
  • Principalnorthwind:finance (declared)
  • Actionpayments.release
  • Authoritydeclared
02 Establish

Humsana separates what is established from what is claimed and what remains unknown.

Each fact carries how it was arrived at: measured by the service, verified against something, reported by the caller, or unknown.

The counts stay visible in the record. In a typical run the service establishes its own facts and leaves the caller's claims as claims.

Establish
  • Established0
  • Asserted5
  • Unresolved3
03 Confirm

The consequence decides whether a person has to approve.

The action class carries a consequence level. Higher-consequence actions require a human step, and the approval is signed over the action rather than over the request.

The approving human is authenticated by the principal's identity system. Humsana verifies what it is handed and records who approved, by what method, over which action.

04 Bind

The approval is tied to the action, its values and its expiry.

The bounded values are fixed at approval. A value that is inside a limit can move within it, and the limit is applied at execution.

The authorization is single use. Whatever happens, it cannot stand for a second action.

05 Enforce

The executor checks immediately before the action runs.

The executor's own rules run first, exactly as they did before. Then the executor presents the action to Humsana and asks whether it is still the action that was authorized.

An exact match proceeds. Bounded variance inside the limits proceeds with the limits applied. A material change is refused, and the refusal names the field, both values and the rule.

07 Measurement

How those numbers were taken.

The authorize path and the enforce path were run against the running service on a single host, 40 actions, and timed at the boundary.

Median authorize 5.8 ms. Median enforce 2.9 ms. Worst case over the 40 actions 12.9 ms.

No claim is made here for the network between a customer's executor and the boundary, or for the cost of the customer's own execution path. Those belong to the integration.

Single host deployment, 40 actions. Re-measure on your own host before you rely on these figures.

08 Refusals

What a refusal says.

Every refusal carries a code. The code names which check failed and, where a field was involved, which field carried the difference.

CodeWhat failed
EFFECTUATION_DRIFTThe presented action differs from the authorized action in at least one material field. The refusal carries the field, the authorized value, the presented value and the rule that was applied.
MAY_NOT_CHANGEThe field is fixed by the authorization and cannot be altered at execution.
MAY_NOT_INCREASEThe field may be reduced but not raised beyond the authorized value.
MAY_ONLY_SHRINKThe field may only reduce the reach of the action that was authorized.
BINDING_MISMATCHThe binding digest presented at execution is not the digest that was approved.
RECEIPT_NOT_FOUNDThere is no authorization with that identifier.
RECEIPT_EXPIREDThe authorization was valid when it was issued and is not valid now.
GRANT_ALREADY_USEDThe authorization was spent. A second presentation does not spend it again.
GRANT_NOT_FOUNDThe grant behind the action cannot be found.
GRANT_DOES_NOT_COVER_THIS_ACTIONThe grant exists and does not cover the action that was presented.
AUTHORIZATION_NOT_GRANTEDThe stored decision is not one that grants anything. A match proves the action is the one that was asked about, never that it was permitted.
CONFIRMATION_NOT_OBTAINEDThe action required a human confirmation and no valid confirmation is bound to it.
ACTION_OUTSIDE_SCOPEThe action class is outside the scope the principal granted.
CONDITION_NOT_EVALUABLEA condition in the authorization cannot be evaluated deterministically, so it is not assumed to hold.
CLOCK_NOT_SUPPLIEDDeciding needs a time and none was supplied.
PROVIDER_DEADLINE_EXCEEDEDAn external check did not answer inside the boundary's deadline, so the boundary did not treat silence as consent.
BOUNDARY_STORE_UNAVAILABLEThe boundary could not read its own store, so it could not answer.

Codes are the names the service returns, read from the implementation.
