With Humsana
The executor trusts that the request is the approved one.
The arriving action is compared with the authorized one, field by field.
An approval is a claim inside the request.
The grant is single use and names what it covers.
A refusal is advice the caller may ignore.
A refusal is a code naming the field and both values, and it spends nothing.
The evidence is the agent's own log.
The record is signed, and a third party verifies it offline.
