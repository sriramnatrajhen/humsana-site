01Mechanism

From authority to action.

What the caller presents, what the boundary establishes, and what it refuses to infer.

01

The request

An agent presents a claim, not a proof: who it acts for, the action as structured fields, and the authority it says it holds. A presented delegation is recorded as unverified today.

The consequence class it declares is recorded. A declaration can never lower what that class requires.

02

Evidence

Five states, never blended: measured, verified, reported, unverified, unknown. Measured outranks reported, which outranks silence.

A claim can make a decision stricter. It can never make one more permissive.

03

Consequence

The action class sets the floor: how much evidence it needs, and whether a person must confirm.

A read or a draft stays cheap. Irreversible actions floor at a person by construction, not by a model's confidence.

04

Confirmation

The approver sees the fields that will be bound and approves that instruction as it stands. The approval is single use, and it expires.

Humsana does not authenticate the approver. That belongs to your identity system.

05

The grant

Every bound field is named with its digest, so a changed byte is a changed field. The bound set may shrink. It may not grow.

A refusal carries the field, both values and the rule as a structure, not as a sentence.

06

Execution

The executor presents the action and its grant immediately before the send, the payment or the deletion. A field that moved stops it, and the grant is not consumed.

A second presentation is refused, so a retry cannot release twice. The rail is yours. Making it require a grant is the integration.

07

The provider

The deployment ships deterministic and runs that way. Where a model is enabled it can only ask for a stricter outcome, and nothing it returns can hold a decision.

The boundary waits for a provider only to its own deadline, and records a timeout by name.

02The calls

Four calls, and a refusal you can read.

POST /v1/authorize/verify decide whether this exact action may proceed POST /v1/authorize/confirm/request ask the authenticated approver POST /v1/authorize/confirm record that approval against that instruction POST /v1/authorize/effectuate release, only if the arriving action matches the grant
