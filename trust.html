What is measured.

On the current single-host deployment, 5.8 ms median to authorize one exact action and 2.9 ms median to check it at execution. Worst case 12.9 ms over a run of 40 actions. The work is a deterministic comparison of bound fields, not a model call.

Agent variance

Seven runs of one autonomous task, on one provider, in one day. Seven distinct artifacts, and the recipient set was the same every time. All 42 presentations were refused before the send API: zero matched, zero grants consumed, and 14 messages that the unwatched executor really sent.

Voice

The noise gate is set at 30 dB. Across the evaluation of 150 clips, 75 recommended PROCEED before the gate and none does now, and the same 75 were returned as human before and none is now. Audio below the floor returns inconclusive rather than cleared.

What is kept, and for how long.

A record holds

What the caller claimed, what was established, the grant with the digest of every bound field, the confirmation with its approver, and the receipt.

Voice audio

Decoded in memory to reach a verdict and then discarded. It is never written to disk, and no voiceprint is built.

Metadata

Kept for 30 days. It contains no audio and no fingerprint of the audio.
