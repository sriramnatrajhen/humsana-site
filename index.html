03How it works

Observe, infer, act.

01

Observe

Keystroke timing, rhythm, scroll velocity, dwell time. Never the content itself.

02

Infer

Stress, focus, fatigue, and hesitation, computed in real time as probabilistic signals.

03

Act

Systems adapt: gate a risky action, adjust an AI response, or surface a well-timed prompt.
