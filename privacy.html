Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

Humsana is built to infer context without capturing content. We design our systems so sensitive data is never collected in the first place.

What we do not collect

  • Keystroke content or text input
  • Messages, prompts, or screen data
  • Audio, video, or biometric identifiers
  • Personal identifiers such as name, email, or phone
  • Credentials, passwords, or tokens

What we observe

Behavioral timing signals only:

  • Keystroke timing intervals, not characters
  • Typing rhythm and correction frequency
  • Scroll velocity and dwell time
  • Interaction and task-switch timing

How data is processed

Developer Tools: entirely on-device. No raw data is transmitted. Local audit logs, if enabled, live in ~/.humsana/.

Partner SDK: signals are processed in real time. Only aggregated, probabilistic indicators are returned. Raw streams are not retained.

What Humsana is not used for

  • Surveillance or monitoring
  • Employee tracking or scoring
  • Psychological or medical diagnosis
  • Law enforcement or intelligence use

Compliance

Designed to align with GDPR and CCPA. Because we avoid collecting personal data by design, compliance risk is minimized.
